**[R1-MUST] Time and publication.** For timed rows, `effective_start_at = starts_at` and `effective_end_at = COALESCE(ends_at, starts_at + interval '2 hours')`. A supplied end must be strictly after the start. For all-day rows, effective bounds are `start_on::timestamp AT TIME ZONE event_timezone` and `end_on::timestamp AT TIME ZONE event_timezone`, with `end_on > start_on` and an exclusive end. Require an IANA timezone and unambiguous local boundary conversions. Timed and all-day representations are mutually exclusive. Use `effective_end_at > p_now` for publication, reinstatement, Discover, new tracking, and calendar eligibility. An ongoing event can be published. The fallback two hours controls visibility only. Display an unknown end as unknown and omit its timed `DTEND` in calendar export.

**[R1-MUST] Other publication invariants.** Published rows need a nonblank title and organizer, a complete schedule, event type, mode, and one primary source. In-person/hybrid rows need an Indian `city_id`. Online rows require `city_id IS NULL` and `online_india_access_confirmed = true`. Accept either an exact deadline or a date-only deadline. An exact deadline may equal or precede the effective start. A date-only deadline may equal or precede the local start date. Both cancellation fields must be present **iff** status is `canceled`. The cancellation action sets the database timestamp and selected reason together. Display the timestamp and a human-readable reason label on the detail page. Reinstate or archive clears the cancellation fields and preserves the audit code. Apply the event state table transitions, source updates, and version increments in one transaction.

**[R1-MUST]** Use created_at and updated_at timestamptz for mutable rows. Audit fields are id, actor_id, target_id, action, prior_version, new_version, recorded_at, reason_code and archived_url_keys. Rate buckets have primary key (scope, key_digest, window_start) and an integer count. Serialize competing mutations for a given tracking key, including creation when no row yet exists.

**[R2-MUST]** Name the feed state fields feed_key (PK), etag, http_last_modified and last_good_at. Feed items have current_fingerprint, reviewed_fingerprint, sequence and source_last_modified_at alongside their candidate and review fields. Set reminder_hold when **any** linked pending proposal changes the schedule or explicitly cancels the event. For job_runs use dispatch_token_digest, dispatch_token_expires_at, dispatch_status, execution_status, started_at, finished_at, error_code and counts. In reminder_deliveries use kind, status, unsubscribe_token_digest, unsubscribe_expires_at, consent_version_at_send and error_code for the corresponding fields in the relation table. Require due_at < expires_at for materialized deliveries. Lease checks include DB time. A recovered lease does not reset the retry horizon.

## Event and tracking state machines [R1-MUST]

**[R1-MUST]** Apply transitions transactionally and audit status changes. `Archived` is terminal. Ended and ongoing are derived time classifications.

| From | Permitted next states | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Draft | Published, Archived | Publish only if validation passes and the effective end is in the future. |
| Published | Draft, Canceled, Archived | Draft unpublishes and keeps source claims. Cancel stores timestamp and reason. Archive deletes source links and releases their keys. |
| Canceled | Published, Archived | Reinstatement requires an explicit action and full publish validation. Clear cancellation fields. Archive releases source claims. |
| Archived | None | Keep the stable event ID for existing tracking rows. A new event may claim the released URL. |

**[R1-MUST]** Other transitions return `409 INVALID_TRANSITION`. Same-state metadata edits use optimistic versions. A canceled detail shows the reason and timestamp. **[R2-MUST]** Unpublish, cancel, or archive suppresses reminder attempts. Keep cancellation visible in My Events. **[LATER-HARDENING]** Defer outbound cancellation notifications.

| Request, evaluated in order | Result |
|---|---|
| `none` | `200`. Delete the caller’s row if present. |
| Exact repeat of caller’s existing Saved or Registered state | `200` with no tracking-row update, irrespective of event status. |
| New or changed state, published with `effective_end_at > p_now` | `200` and apply the change. |
| New or changed state, ended or canceled | `409 EVENT_INACTIVE`. |
| New or changed state, draft, archived, or unknown | `404`. Preserve any existing row. |

## Reminder eligibility [R2-MUST]

**[R2-MUST]** Use the first matching row. `*` means any value. A date-only deadline is non-exact. Let **D** be the exact deadline, **S** the effective start, and **E** the effective end, including the two-hour visibility fallback for timed events with unknown ends. For attendance, target **T** is the timed start or 09:00 in the event timezone on the first all-day date. `due_at = anchor - interval '24 hours'`. `expires_at = LEAST(cutoff, E) - interval '15 minutes'`. The window is `due_at <= p_now AND p_now < expires_at`. Deadline cutoff is **D**. Attendance cutoff is **S**, including for ongoing events and all-day listings.

| Tracking | Status | Opt-in | Deadline knowledge | Window or extra condition | Eligible? | Anchor | Due | Expiry | Reason if ineligible |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| None | `*` | `*` | `*` | `*` | No | — | — | — | Untracked |
| Saved/Registered | Draft, canceled, archived | `*` | `*` | `*` | No | — | — | — | Inactive status |
| Saved/Registered | Published | Off | `*` | `*` | No | — | — | — | Opted out |
| Saved/Registered | Published | On | `*` | Hold, deletion pending, unverified recipient, or active suppression | No | — | — | — | Applicable hold |
| Saved | Published | On | Missing/date-only | `*` | No | — | — | — | No exact deadline |
| Saved | Published | On | Exact | Registration closed | No | — | — | — | Registration closed |
| Saved | Published | On | Exact | Before due | No | D | D − 24h | min(D,E) − 15m | Too early |
| Saved | Published | On | Exact | In window | Yes | D | D − 24h | min(D,E) − 15m | — |
| Saved | Published | On | Exact | At/after expiry | No | D | D − 24h | min(D,E) − 15m | Too late |
| Registered | Published | On | `*` | Before due | No | T | T − 24h | min(S,E) − 15m | Too early |
| Registered | Published | On | `*` | In window | Yes | T | T − 24h | min(S,E) − 15m | — |
| Registered | Published | On | `*` | At/after expiry | No | T | T − 24h | min(S,E) − 15m | Too late |

**[R2-MUST]** Eligibility is separate from delivery history. A new eligible user inside the window can send on the next tick. An accepted key cannot send again. A confirmed new target produces a new key. Include exact due and expiry boundaries, timed unknown ends, all-day midnight cutoff, ongoing publication, and both tracking transitions in the common fixtures.

## Source links [R1-MUST]

1. **[R1-MUST]** Accept absolute HTTPS URLs up to 2,048 characters on standard port 443. Reject credentials, IP literals, localhost, control characters, and signed-access parameters such as `access_token`, `signature`, `x-amz-*`, and `x-goog-*`. Require the actual HTTPS link for HTTP input.
2. **[R1-MUST]** Normalize hostname to lowercase ASCII, omit port 443, resolve dot segments, and normalize percent escapes without decoding reserved characters. Preserve path case and non-root trailing slashes. Treat `lu.ma` and `luma.com` as distinct hosts.
3. **[R1-MUST]** Strip case-insensitive `utm_*`, `fbclid`, `gclid`, `dclid`, `msclkid`, `mc_cid`, and `mc_eid`. Preserve other query values and repeated parameters. Sort query parameters for `url_key` only when names are unique. Keep safe fragments in `public_url`; drop ordinary anchors from `url_key` and retain router fragments beginning `/` or `!/`.
4. **[R1-MUST]** Enforce unique source keys in Postgres. Transactional archive removes all source-link rows. Unpublish to Draft preserves links. Test archive and republish a new UUID with the same link, and verify a concurrent claim yields one winner. **[R2-MUST]** Apply separate, stricter rules to the configured feed endpoint.

**[R1-MUST]** Archive records normalized keys in private audit for 180 days. Only live source rows participate in uniqueness. A repeated link on the same event succeeds. A competing event gets `409 SOURCE_URL_CONFLICT` and the conflicting event is visible to its curator. Before publishing, warn on normalized title trigram similarity ≥0.65, starts within 24 hours and same city or both online. Normalize using NFKC, lowercase, punctuation to spaces and whitespace collapse. Link the existing listing or audit the distinctness reason.

## Public catalog and private tracking

- **[R1-MUST] Filters.** Accept one value per facet in `/discover` and `GET /api/catalog/discover`: `q`, `type`, `mode`, `price`, `source`, `city`, `distance`, `sort`, and `cursor`. AND facets. A source match uses any visible source link. Hybrid matches Online and In person. A distance filter requires a city and applies to in-person/hybrid rows. `mode=online` combined with distance returns `400`. Nearby is `ST_DWithin(city.center, origin.center, 50000)`. Farther away is its complement for eligible physical listings. Label the result as a city-center estimate. Unknown price never matches Free or Paid.
- **[R1-MUST] Search.** GIN-index `to_tsvector('english', title || organizer || summary)` with null handling and spaces between fields. Query with `websearch_to_tsquery('english', q)`, capped at 100 characters. For plain word queries, also match the last token as a stemmed prefix when it has at least three characters and yields a single lexeme. Bind inputs and retain whole-lexeme matches for earlier words. Test `hackathons` → `hackathon`, the reverse, `hack` → `hackathon`, and quoted and negated input.
- **[R1-MUST] Ordering and paging.** Return up to 24 cards and `nextCursor`, using one extra row to determine continuation. Start sort is ongoing first, then effective start ascending, then UUID ascending. Deadline sort is future, exact, non-closed deadlines ascending, other deadlines `NULLS LAST`, then effective start and UUID ascending. Use a plain base64url JSON keyset cursor capped at 1 KiB decoded, containing canonical filter identity, `asOf`, and the typed last tuple. Reject filter mismatches, malformed tuples, future times, and ages over 15 minutes with `400 CURSOR_INVALID`. Pick first-page `asOf` from the database clock. Omit a total count. Status-changing actions and details use live data.
- **[R1-MUST] Web delivery.** Use a static, session-independent Discover shell. A Client Component loads public catalog JSON and separately loads the signed-in user’s tracking state. The successful JSON response has `Cache-Control: public, max-age=0, must-revalidate` and `Vercel-CDN-Cache-Control: public, s-maxage=30`. Fetch public JSON with credentials omitted. The route ignores cookies, emits no `Set-Cookie`, bypasses Auth session refresh, and sends no shared-cache directive on errors. Public detail, calendar eligibility, and private APIs read live with `no-store`. A recently changed card can linger in Discover for one cache interval. Opening it or acting on it checks current state. Verify `MISS` then `HIT` on the same staging URL and no user-specific data in the cached response. [Vercel CDN cache](https://vercel.com/docs/caching/cdn-cache)
- **[R1-MUST] CSP and metadata.** The static shell and public detail use a fixed CSP allowing first-party scripts and the inline scripts Next.js needs for hydration. Accept the weaker inline-script protection on those public pages and render all provider/feed strings as escaped plain text. Sign-in, settings, My Events, and curator pages use nonce CSP and private `no-store`. Deny framing and objects, use HSTS on HTTPS, `nosniff`, and a restrictive referrer policy. Allow Turnstile origins only where needed. Set canonical detail URLs and text Open Graph metadata. Set `/discover` canonical for its permutations and use query-matched `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, follow` for filtered or paginated URLs. Sitemap contains unfiltered Discover and active published details. Mark hidden, private, canceled, past, and nonproduction pages `noindex`. [Next.js CSP](https://nextjs.org/docs/app/guides/content-security-policy), [query-matched headers](https://nextjs.org/docs/app/api-reference/config/next-config-js/headers)
- **[R1-MUST] Tracking reads and writes.** My Events reads the caller’s `user_events` rows under RLS and left-joins visible catalog rows. Past shows **Ended**. Canceled shows **Canceled**. Unpublished and archived rows show **Event unavailable** with Remove. For an authenticated request, check the caller’s existing row first. `none` removes it or succeeds if absent. An exact repeat of an existing Saved or Registered state returns `200` with no tracking-row update, even if the listing is ended, canceled, or hidden. Any new row or change between states requires a currently active published event. Follow the tracking state table for errors.

- **[R1-MUST] Display and performance.** Show timed online events in IST and also show the labeled event zone when different. Preserve all-day local dates. With 1,000 fixture events on staging, target p95 catalog SQL ≤150 ms across 50 representative calls, mobile Lighthouse LCP ≤2.5 s in four of five runs, and no horizontal overflow at 320 px.

## Row security and RPC contracts

**[R1-MUST]** Enable RLS on every relation and revoke default table, column and PUBLIC function grants. `owner` below means `auth.uid() IS NOT NULL AND user_id = auth.uid()`. `eligible` means an account_controls row for that UID has adult_confirmed_at present and deletion_requested_at null. Implement private checks with qualified, narrowly privileged helpers to avoid recursive policies. There are no browser table-write grants. Public event/source policies expose only approved output columns.

## Authorized feed [R2-MUST]

**[R2-MUST] Connection and schedule.** Poll one configured organizer-approved HTTPS `.ics` endpoint at minute 02 every sixth UTC hour. Configure exact permitted host, URL, IANA default timezone for all-day records, and approved fields. Resolve and reject non-global addresses, including private and IPv4-mapped private ranges, then connect to a validated address with the approved hostname for SNI and certificate checking. Repeat on each poll. Accept UTF-8 and identity encoding only. Ignore embedded links and attachments for fetching. A run is claimed once and finishes within a 60-second function budget, far shorter than the poll interval. One feed and one scheduled slot remove the need for a separate feed lease service.

**[R2-MUST] Parse and review.** Send `If-None-Match` and `If-Modified-Since` from the last good response. A `304` refreshes job freshness. A failed or malformed response preserves last-good state. Flag recurrence, floating times, unsupported custom TZIDs, and conflicting duplicate UIDs. Ignore descriptions, HTML, attendees, and meeting credentials. Fingerprint approved normalized UID, title, time representation, timezone, location, event URL, and cancellation. Only a fully parsed `200` snapshot marks missing UIDs. A missing item alone does not cancel or hold an event.

**[R2-MUST] Atomic approval.** A pending explicit cancellation or material schedule change for a linked event sets `reminder_hold`. **[R2-MUST] Claim and send.** Materialize due work with the unique ledger key. Claim at most ten rows using `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, a 90-second lease, and an incremented attempt number. Finalize only the still-leased row with that attempt number. Recheck reminder eligibility table, latest tracking and consent, feed hold, account deletion, suppression, and current verified recipient immediately before sending. The delivery UUID is the provider idempotency key. Save `may_have_sent=true` before dispatch. A definitive rejection restores the prior ambiguity flag, a timeout leaves it set, and an acceptance records the provider ID. Retry transient network errors, `429`, and `5xx` after 5, 15, and 60 minutes, with a longer provider delay only if it fits before expiry and within 23 hours of first attempt. Retain an ambiguous outcome as `unknown` once no safe retry remains. Clear the snapshot on terminal completion.

**[R2-MUST] Delivery states.** Use `pending`, `leased`, `retry`, `accepted`, `expired`, `suppressed`, `failed`, and `unknown`. Accepted rows never reopen. Changing tracking, opting out, or deleting an account prevents a new attempt after the next eligibility check. An in-flight provider call can finish. Untracking preserves the delivery row. Account deletion cascades it.

**[R2-MUST]** Verify the raw webhook body, signature and timestamp with the team’s webhook secret. Match sender, provider message ID and current verified recipient. Use a keyed digest of the normalized recipient. On exact Permanent bounce upsert expiry at provider event time + 90 days. On complaint use +180 days. Take the later stored expiry on conflict.
